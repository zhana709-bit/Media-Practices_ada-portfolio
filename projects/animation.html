About the project

I have long wanted to experiment with the spinning-disc format of the phenakistoscope, one of the earliest animation devices. In EV Ballet, I connect its circular motion with the turns and repetition of ballet, bringing EV into a playful dance loop. Contrasting textures and color palettes distinguish EV from the background, creating separate visual layers while keeping the dancing character in focus.
