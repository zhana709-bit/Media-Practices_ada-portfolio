SOUND WORK / 2026

Annoying Ev ASMR

A looping sound narrative about work, interruptions, and life with my cat, EV.

Discipline
Sound Art
Year
2026
Tools
Audacity

About the project

My creative practice centers on my cat, EV. Annoying Ev ASMR turns a familiar routine—working at my computer, responding to EV’s demands for attention, and preparing its food—into a looping sound narrative. As the ending returns to the beginning, small interruptions become moments of connection and companionship.

Process

The story developed around the sounds I could capture: EV was not always cooperative, and background noise often complicated the recordings. Working in Audacity, I used editing, noise reduction, and volume adjustments to shape these recordings into a coherent sequence. The loop emerged during this process, giving the piece a sense of completion while reflecting the recurring rhythm of our everyday life.
