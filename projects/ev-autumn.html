About the project

This photo scene sticker kit shares the feeling of having EV by your side in everyday life. Autumn photographs become little scenes for EV to explore, while white-bordered character stickers invite people to place him into their own stories. Packaged together in a clear bag, the photo prints and stickers turn small seasonal moments into playful experiences of companionship.
